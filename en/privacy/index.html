Privacy policy

This site collects as little about you as possible. Here's what is processed anyway, and why.

Who is responsible

Henrik Becker Consulting AB (Org. nr 559120-2147, registered in Lidingö, Sweden) is the data controller. Send questions about your privacy to henrik [at] becker-consulting.se.

When you visit the site

The site sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking service and stores nothing in your browser. The fonts are served from the same server as the pages, so reading the site sends no requests to third parties.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address and which pages you fetch, and keeps them for a short time for operations and security. That is covered by GitHub's privacy statement. We receive no data about individual visitors.

When you contact us

If you email us, we keep the email and whatever details you send yourself for as long as we need them to reply and for any assignment that follows. If it becomes an assignment, contracts and invoices are kept for as long as Swedish bookkeeping law requires.

Ads

This site shows no ads. The Google AdSense tag in the page source only shows that the domain belongs to our AdSense account, and loads nothing. Ads are shown in the Dopp app, which has its own privacy policy and cookie policy.

Links to other sites

Links to sites such as henrikbecker.net, GitHub and LinkedIn lead to pages with their own terms and privacy policies.

Your rights

You have the right to know what data we hold about you, to have mistakes corrected and to have data deleted once we no longer need it. Get in touch with us first. You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection, IMY.
